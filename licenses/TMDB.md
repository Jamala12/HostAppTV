# TMDB attribution

The TMDB logo in `app/src/main/res/drawable/tmdb_logo.xml` preserves the geometry,
colours and aspect ratio of TMDB’s approved Primary short (blue) SVG, retrieved
19 October 2026 from [the official asset](https://www.themoviedb.org/assets/2/v4/logos/v2/blue_square_1-5bdc75aaebeb75dc7ae79426ddd9be3b2be1e342510f8202baf6bffa71d7f5c4.svg).

TMDB’s logo and third-party movie/series artwork are not covered by this
repository’s MIT code licence. Use them according to [TMDB’s attribution and
brand guidance](https://www.themoviedb.org/about/logos-attribution).

The app’s About section displays the approved logo and required notice:
“This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.”
